Amongst the different roleplay games I played, the longest I have kept a character (surprisingly ) was for *Call of Ctuhlu*. This is a very dangerous universe, where your characters have no power at all, and are confronted to realities far beyond their comprehensions… Our Game Master was so good at creating mysteries mixing **scientific and esoteric** worlds, it really kept us all on the edge for many evenings. 

After investigating different **mysteries**, our characters were obviously damaged in one way or another, psychologically or physically, but alive. I used to play with my group of friends in London before I left, so I wanted to make a piece to celebrate our adventures !
