**Boss Fight** is a tribute to my favourite Actual Play. *Table Quest* is a series, involving streamers who gather around a table to play *Dungeon and Dragons*. I discovered it with the third season and I really fell in love with the table, wether it was the characters, the story, but also the chemistry between the players and the Game Master. 

When the season ended on a big epic combat in an underground vault against an **mindflayer-controlled undead dragon**, I really wanted to make a big piece for it. I had done a few other illustrations with those characters before but this one is probably the most ambitious. I am waiting for the next season to make more !
