When my brother told me he was working on a video game on his spare time, and he asked me if I wanted to help him designing some characters , I didn’t really hesitate. He has been building a whole universe for years, with really cool influences of post-apocalyptic, aetherpunk and Ancient Middle Eastern aesthetics.

As his name suggests, *the Lieutenant* has spent a lifelong fighting, he is an amazing marksman despite his blind eye, thanks to his little Familiar that helps him by sharing **sight** with him.

This is such a nice opportunity for me to practice character design, and I am really honoured to be able to help my brother with his project, I can’t wait for the game to take shape !
