Some time ago, I fell into a new rabbit hole and started to learn about the lore and universe of *Warhammer 40k*. I am still very much scratching the surface, but out of all the Imperium’s factions, the **Grey Knights** might be my favourite. They really have this dark paladin aura that I find really cool, ready to anihilate entire worlds to preserve the secrecy of their existence, and push the notion of “sacrifice for the greater good” to the extreme. 
 
Drawing for *Warhammer* is a good training for me, as it involves both a mix of fantasy and sci-fi visual look, forcing me to go out of my comfort zone in terms of design. I had this idea of **Grey Knights**, descending on a world to purge it from a demonic incursion and just burning it down to the grounds to get rid of all the potential witnesses and corrupted souls.
